In a recent development, A funny thing happens when you are styling your house for a magazine. Your “normal holiday brain” (I think we know mine is already very, very questionable) goes into overdrive because, honestly, magazines don’t want “normal”. They want new fun ideas that people can glean and be inspired by (both small and large), so while I typically only put a couple alpine trees in here for nighttime holiday cozy vibes and some red pillows/blankets, I spread some holiday cheer here more than usual with a very easy holiday decor hack. AND I LOVED IT.

Table Lamps | Red Pillows | Blue Pillow | Stripe Lumbar | Light Blue Pillow.

Industry observers note that as you can see, we “wrapped” all the vintage seascapes in our DIY’d ripped fabric ribbon, which honestly was so easy once you have the materials (fabric, scissors, tape). For some of the smaller ones, we actually wrapped it all the way around, like a present, and for the larger ones, we use blue tape on the back to adhere each “leg” to the back. In case you are upcoming to my janky ribbon hack, here’s what I do when I want 6″ wide ribbon. I buy yardage of fabric (like 2 yards) and then rip/cut it super wide. Do they sometimes fray on the side? Sure. But by doing this, you have way more selection (fun patterns, textures, and just FUN) and it’s WAYYY easier to keep for next year, while being way more affordable (wide ribbon is crazy expensive). Sometimes I have to snip the fray, and if you are a perfectionist, this might bug you, but it’s barely noticeable (especially when you live in a holiday explosion – literally no one can see the fray through our level of cheer.
