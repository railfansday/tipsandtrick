Recent findings suggest that 1. Gather some gold and silver glitter together into a plastic container with a lid. This is much easier than sprinkling onto the glued surface.

In a recent development, 2. Next, go to The Graphics Fairy to download and save some of her free gorgeous vintage Christmas images. She has so many amazing choices! (Related: Anthropologie and Terrain Just Made Christmas Decoratin...)

In a recent development, 3. Print them onto ,matte white card stock. For easy printing, copy and paste the image into a word document about 5 x 7 in size. You can also send these to the print store or print them at the library if you don’t have a good quality at home printer.

Recent findings suggest that 4. Cut them out rounding the corners slightly.

5. Line the edges with glue. You can either squeeze it straight from the tube use a paint brush to apply it to the paper. Then dip  your wet glued edges into your glitter container. This gives them a more vintage look. Shake off and let dry flat.

Then you can. hang, display or craft with them! They are sweet framed in a frame or even decoupaged on a plaque.

Industry observers note that I even added some to my Paris artwork pieces in the dining room. Just put double-sided tape on the backs of the cards and then stick right on the glass.

They look great displayed on my kids’ artwork display ladder.
