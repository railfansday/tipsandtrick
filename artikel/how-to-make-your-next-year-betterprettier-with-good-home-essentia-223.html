In a recent development, to me, Black Friday is all about planning for the future and making “future you” less stressed while saving money. That future could be as soon as getting presents for the holidays in order, or buying things for your home now to make 2027 as comfortable and fresh as possible. Since we are a home design blog first and foremost, we’ll be focusing on the latter today:) We’ve talked about Wayfair a lot over the years, and it’s for good reason – they basically sell everything you need when it comes to your home. They also have truly great prices, which only get better during a sale like Black Friday. So I went through a ton of their sale items and found really good home essentials to make your home feel really put together before we enter into a new year. It’s sort of like a home resolutions list to check off early!

Roborock QV 35S Robot Vacuum and Mop | ProHeat 2X Revolution Pet Pro Plus.

Recent findings suggest that while I don’t have a pet myself, I have many friends and family (my brother has two Great Pyrenees mixes) who do, and the shedding is no joke. A great vacuum and a carpet pet cleaner are a must. My brother has a robot vacuum like this one for daily control, and it makes all of the difference. A perfect “set and forget” system that they love (the gift that keeps on giving). Of course, you don’t need a pet to want a great robot vacuum. I may need to invest in one so I can enjoy clean floors with way less personal labor:) Oh, and this one has a mop function, which is the real game-changer! Now for those with carpet or upholstery and pets, this ProHeat 2X Revolution Pet Pro Plus will stop making stain removal a nightmare. The reviews are great (over 9k with 4.5 stars). I think getting new bed pillows and a comforter insert are incredible gifts to give yourself and/or your family. A perfect reset for a new year, right? All three of these have rave reviews and are at truly great prices. I’m super interested in that cooling pillow. Sounds like a dream!

Modern Floating Shelves (Set of 2) | Pre-Assembled Solid Wood Pull Out Cabinet Organizer Drawer.

According to recent reports, I know I’m not alone when I say hunkering down and organizing at the beginning of the year is my absolute favorite. Wait till spring? No, freaking way. Anyway, latest shelving may be something that’s super helpful in your newly, perfectly organized home (especially after all the “no longer needed” items are in the trash or donated). Or if making your kitchen simply more functional is what will make your year better and easier, here are some beautiful pre-assembled cabinet pull-out drawers for you. Emily is a huge kitchen drawer proponent, but if you already have lower cabinets, this is a way to get the best of both worlds.

Cuisinart Air Fryer + Convection Toaster Oven |  Countertop Nugget Ice Maker.

While I’m not trying to push more small appliances you don’t need, IF you are like me with limited counter space, an 8-1 counter air fryer and oven sounds like the dream. It’s also nice when you typically only cook for 1 to 2 ppl. Again, great reviews, and Cuisinart is definitely a trusted brand. Then is this the year you have nugget ice on hand 24/7? With this ice maker, that could be a reality:) It’s a steep discount and will likely make yours the most popular house on the block!

Modern Ceiling Fan | Nicola LED Ceiling Fan.

I know it’s finally been cooling down, but you could get yourself set up well in time for the warm weather next year (and beyond) with a modern and stylish ceiling fan. It might be time for you to swap out the old one and grab one of these on sale.

Chada Rattan Shoe Storage Cabinet | Clie Shoe Storage Cabinet.

Sources indicate that I’ve talked many times about entryway furniture, but it’s such a gift to yourself to walk into your home and have a clutter-free space. You feel a sigh of relief when you get home instead of instant stress from a mess. Both of these cabinets are for shoe storage, which I don’t think you can ever get enough of, right?

Elfriede Farmhouse Jute Blend Area Rug.

Don’t underestimate the power of a rug. That could be in your entry, hallway, living room, kitchen; the list goes on. This rug looks like it’s not going to show any dirt and has over 10k reviews of almost 5 stars! It’s the perfect affordable rug that looks great and can take a lot of “life”!

Modern Wood TV Stand | Washed Linen Textured Pillow Cover (Set of 2).

These get even more into the decor realm. Proper storage is truly such a great gift that we can give to ourselves and for some many of us, our living/TV rooms can use it. They are often cluttered with toys, books, random electronics, and a proper (and affordable) media cabinet can be the answer to ease your overstimulated eyes. For a much smaller, but sometimes just as effective option, some fresh throw pillows. It’s an injection of newness that can truly change the feeling of a room. I like these pillow covers because the color is great and the fringe adds the perfect amount of “fun” while still being classic.

Sabine Metal Rounded Rectangle Wall Mirror | Modern Scalloped Corner Mirror.

Much like the pillow covers, something as simple as a vanity mirror can make your bathroom feel new! These are both “simple but special” and a good price. But these aren’t exclusively for bathrooms. These are great for entries or over a dresser!

Metal Upholstered Dining Chairs (Set of 2) | Dalpat Iron Arm Chair.

Sources indicate that if you want more comfortable dining chairs, here are two upholstered modern ones. They’ll inject newness instantly for a slammin deal.

Liberty 6 – Drawer Small Dresser | Celine 2-Drawer Nightstand.

Let’s finish out with some more storage, but for the bedroom. I think both of these pieces are so pretty and offer so much function. The dresser is on the smaller side, so it’s great for a small room, a guest room, or a kids’ room. The style of the drawer fronts is classic while feeling really modern too. And how good are the legs? It’s just a very sweet piece. Then this nightstand is so versatile; it’s chic but not trying too hard, and it offers open AND closed storage. What’s not to love? If you need a good nightstand, I think this is a top contender. Oh, and it comes in EIGHT other colors.

I hope that if you sometimes need a nudge to plan ahead to make your next year better and prettier with good home essentials (all while saving money), this post was that for you. Happy holidays and happy Black Friday shopping!
