I have been THROUGH THE RINGER on the hair loss stuff and am finally exiting the other side the last few years (I’m 47). Here’s what’s worked best for me personally… your mileage may vary:.

-Getting my ferritin levels up to over 50 or even 70 if possible however you can (work with a medical professional) -Getting one of those scalp/shampoo massager thingys (rubber, with the pointy ends) and massaging my scalp for 5 minutes a night. I seem to have poor circulation which was affecting my hair -The Ordinary’s hair density serum, I put it on my hairline and temples a few times a week at night -A fully thyroid panel, hypothyroidism, even subclinical, can affect hair loss -Quitting gluten (I tested and I’m not celiac, but I still seem to be sensitive and I could tell its affects on my hair loss) -Lots of protein and healthy fat.

According to recent reports, my ferritin is half what it should be and my doctor (even after I pushed them on it) didn’t seem that concerned. It’s so frustrating because I have other obvious symptoms of low ferritin but I’m working with a dietitian to help with that, as well. I used to have one of those scalp massagers but not sure where it ended up. Interesting!

According to recent reports, removing caffeine (all of it, sorry) and gluten helped my ferritin absorption a lot. Also The American Society of Hematology recently updated its guidelines on ferritin and iron deficiency saying it needs to be much stricter and lower levels are not okay, you may want to share that with your doctor. Good luck!
